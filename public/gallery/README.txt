GALLERY

Each member can have up to 10 gallery images in data/members.json:

"gallery": [
  "/gallery/member-1.jpg",
  "/gallery/member-2.jpg"
]

Use JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, or other browser-supported image files in public/gallery.
Only the first 10 entries are displayed.

The template member contains 10 placeholder SVG entries that you can replace/remove.
